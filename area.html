Learning to drive around {{ site.main_town }}

The area is a good place to learn because it has a bit of everything. The estates in Greylees and Quarrington are quiet enough for first lessons. {{ site.main_town }} town centre gives you traffic lights, mini-roundabouts, one-way streets and the level crossings. The bypass and the A17 and A15 let you practise bigger roundabouts and faster roads, and the lanes out towards the Raucebys, Wilsford and Ancaster are where you learn to handle country roads.
