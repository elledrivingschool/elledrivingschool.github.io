This notice explains what happens to the details you send to {{ site.title }}.

What is collected

If you use the contact form, I receive your name, email address, phone number and whatever you write in your message. If you call or email, I have the number or address you contacted me from.

What it is used for

Only to reply to your enquiry and, if you book, to arrange your lessons. Your details are not sold, shared for marketing or added to a mailing list.

Who handles it

The contact form is delivered by Formspree, a form-handling service, which passes your message to me by email. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details I hold about you, or ask me to correct or delete them, at any time. Email {{ site.email }} or call {{ site.phone_display }}.
