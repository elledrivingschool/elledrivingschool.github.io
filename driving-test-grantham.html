{{ site.main_town }} does not have a driving test centre. Practical tests for my pupils are taken at {{ site.test_centre }}, which is reached along the A153 through Wilsford and Ancaster.

Getting to know {{ site.test_centre }}

You will not be seeing {{ site.test_centre }} for the first time on test day. As your driving comes on, we spend lessons there so that the roads, roundabouts and junctions around the test centre are familiar. This is one of the reasons my lessons are {{ site.lesson_length }} long: there is time to drive there, practise properly and drive back.

What happens in the practical test

The car driving test is the same wherever you take it. It lasts about 40 minutes of driving and has five parts:

  1. Eyesight check. You read a number plate from 20 metres.
  2. "Show me, tell me" questions. One "tell me" question about the car before you set off, and one "show me" question while you are driving.
  3. General driving. You drive on a variety of roads, following the examiner's directions, and pull over and move off again several times.
  4. Reversing. One manoeuvre: parallel parking, parking in a bay, or pulling up on the right and reversing.
  5. Independent driving. About 20 minutes following a sat nav or road signs.

You pass if you make no more than 15 driving faults and no serious or dangerous faults. We practise every part of this in lessons, including the "show me, tell me" questions.

Try it first with a mock test

A mock test is a full practice run under test conditions, marked the way an examiner would mark it. It is the best way to find out whether you are ready, and it takes away the fear of the unknown. I recommend at least one before your real test.
