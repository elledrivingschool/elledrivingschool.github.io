Your instructor

I am a DVSA Approved Driving Instructor, which means I am registered with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency and checked by them to make sure my teaching stays up to standard. I work on my own, so you have the same instructor and the same car for every lesson.

I am based in {{ site.base_detail }}, and teach in {{ site.main_town }} and the villages around it.

How I teach

Lessons go at your pace. We make sure the basics are solid before moving on, because that is what makes everything after it easier, and it is why so many of my pupils go to their test feeling ready.

A lot of the people I teach are nervous to begin with, or have had a bad experience with another instructor. I would rather you asked the same question five times than sat there worrying about it. There is usually a fair bit of laughing in the car as well.

The car

You will learn in a manual {{ site.car | split: ',' | first }} fitted with dual controls, so I can step in if needed while you are finding your feet. It is a small car with good all-round visibility, which makes it easy to judge where the corners are when you are parking and manoeuvring.

If you pass your test in a manual car, your licence covers automatic cars too.
