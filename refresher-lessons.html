Passing your test and feeling confident on the road are two different things. Refresher lessons are for drivers who already hold a full licence and want to feel at ease behind the wheel again.

Who refresher lessons are for

  • You passed years ago and have barely driven since.
  • You have had a long break from driving and are about to need the car again.
  • You have had a knock or a scare and lost your confidence.
  • You avoid certain things, such as roundabouts, dual carriageways, parking or driving at night.
  • You are moving to the area and want to get used to the local roads.

What we cover

Whatever you need. In the first lesson we go for a drive on quiet roads so I can see how you are getting on, and then we agree what to work on. That might be the roundabouts on the {{ site.main_town }} bypass, joining the A17 and A15, parallel parking in town, or simply getting used to being in the driving seat again.

There is no test and no pressure. I have been teaching since {{ site.qualified_year }} and many of my pupils have been nervous drivers.
