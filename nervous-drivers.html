If the thought of driving makes your stomach turn, you are in good company. Plenty of my pupils felt exactly the same on their first lesson.

Some had never driven and had put it off for years. Others had tried with another instructor, been shouted at or rushed, and decided driving was not for them. They went on to pass, many of them first time.

How lessons work if you are nervous

  • We start on quiet roads and only move on when you are ready.
  • You can ask anything, as many times as you need to.
  • The car has dual controls, so I can always step in.
  • Lessons are {{ site.lesson_length }} long, which gives you time to settle before we try anything new.
  • You have the same instructor and the same car every time.
  • You can take a mock test before the real one, so you know what to expect.

I have been teaching since {{ site.qualified_year }}. Nerves are normal, and they do pass.
